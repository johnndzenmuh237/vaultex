Loan terms & conditions

Overview: Loan applications are subject to verification, approval, and regulatory compliance. Borrowers must provide accurate information and meet eligibility requirements.

Loan agreement
By submitting a loan application, the borrower confirms that all provided information is accurate and may be verified. All approved loans must be repaid under the terms outlined in the agreement.
Repayment terms
Borrower agrees to repay the full principal amount plus applicable interest and service fees within the specified repayment schedule. Late or missed payments may result in penalties.
Interest & fees
Interest rates vary based on loan product, duration, and borrower qualification. Late fees or processing charges may apply for overdue installments or failed debits.
Default & recovery
Defaulting may lead to collection procedures, legal action, account restrictions, or reporting to financial authorities. Recovery steps follow the obligations stated in the agreement.
Privacy & data protection
Personal and financial information is used exclusively for loan evaluation, verification, and regulatory compliance. Data is handled securely, following KYC/AML standards.
Approval conditions
Loan approval is not guaranteed. Additional identification, income verification, or documentation may be required before loan funds are disbursed.
Collateral confirmation

To ensure responsible lending and guarantee repayment security, Vaultex requires a refundable security deposit to be locked inside your account for the duration of the loan. This collateral remains fully yours and is never used or deducted unless repayment is not completed.

The collateral amount is a small percentage of the approved loan and is held only as a protection measure. Your deposit stays secured in your account and becomes automatically unlocked on your due payment day once full repayment is confirmed.

Borrowers may be required to upload verification documents such as ID and income proof. These help maintain transparency, security, and regulatory compliance throughout the loan process.

Providing collateral increases approval chances, reduces lender risk, and allows access to larger loan limits.
