How transfers work

1

Check your balance

You can transfer up to your available loan balance. Requests waiting for approval are held, so they are not counted twice.

2

Admin review

Your request is checked by our team. You will see it as Pending in your history.

3

Approved or rejected

If approved, the amount leaves your loan balance and the transfer is marked successful. If rejected, you will get a message explaining why and what to do next. Nothing is deducted.
